ReservationsTick the box when the table is held

Six trains need a seat.

Book these. Tap the rest.
Five of them are JR. Book those now, three seats together. Use JR-EAST Train Reservation, not the Japanese eki-net site. Ordinary reserved seats. If the foreign card fails, take the reservation number to the JR ticket office at Ueno or Tokyo and ask them to print the tickets. Paper is the calm version: each ticket shows the car and the seat. Insert it at the gate, take it back when it comes out, and do that again if there is a second gate. Skyliner is a different company. Buy that one at the airport.
