What to order
15:00–16:30 in Japan.

That is 1:00–2:30 AM US Eastern in November. Awake window: 8:30–9:30 PM Eastern, which is 10:30–11:30 AM the next morning in Tokyo. Do not call during dinner, 18:00–21:00 Japan time.

Use the 03 number.

050 numbers often fail from the United States. Web forms work at any hour. Conveyor sushi does not take a reservation — those shops are on the next page.
