# いのちへの道 — Roman's Road Gospel Tract

A bilingual (日本語 / English) Roman's Road gospel tract designed to feel like a keepsake—not a throwaway flyer.

## Open the preview

```bash
# from this folder
python3 -m http.server 8765
# then visit http://localhost:8765
```

Or simply open `index.html` in a browser.

## Controls

- **日本語面 / English side** — switch faces  
- **Flip** — animate the card turn (`F` key)  
- **Print** — A5 duplex layout (`P` key)

## Content (Roman's Road)

| Step | Theme | Verse |
|------|--------|-------|
| 1 | Sin | Romans 3:23 |
| 2 | Death / gift of life | Romans 6:23 |
| 3 | Christ's love | Romans 5:8 |
| 4 | Believe & call | Romans 10:9, 10:13 |

## Larger A5 tract print

- **Size:** A5 (148 × 210 mm) via `index.html` Print
- **Duplex:** Flip on short edge — Japanese face out when handing to Japanese speakers
- **Stock:** Soft white / cool ivory uncoated, 157–200 gsm
- **Finish:** Matte or soft-touch (avoid glossy flyer look)

## Design notes

- Brand mark: large **道** (the Way) as the first-viewport hero
- Palette: indigo ink (`藍`) + vermillion (`朱`) on cool washi paper
- Typography: Shippori Mincho + Noto Sans JP / Cormorant Garamond
- Motif: a single drawn path — the road itself
